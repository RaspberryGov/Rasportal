Important Protocol Note

The Bitcoin ETF on the TRON network is a decentralized, tokenized smart contract rather than a corporate entity or organization. The smart contract automatically distributes annual dividends to staked positions in the form of additional Bitcoin ETF shares. Consequently, new share issuance is strictly limited to this automated staking mechanism.

Overview & Mechanics

Contributions and redemptions dynamically adjust the underlying Bitcoin reserves and total ETF shares outstanding. Ownership of these shares provides direct, self-executing access to a proportional share of the liquidity pool's total Bitcoin ($BTC) reserves on the TRON network, alongside annualized dividend distributions paid to staked positions in additional shares. Furthermore, all liquidity fees accrued by Freeberry are reinvested to systematically increase the aggregate Bitcoin reserves of the ETF.
